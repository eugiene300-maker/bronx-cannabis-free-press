Bronx Dispensary Map 2026: Every Licensed Store, Neighborhood by Neighborhood

Twenty-three legal stores across four corners of the borough, and the quick checks that separate them from the smoke shops.

Key takeaways

  • The Bronx has 23 open licensed adult-use dispensaries as of late August 2026: 17 CAURD equity stores and six standard retail.
  • The East Bronx has the most stores (eight); Highbridge, Morrisania, Soundview and City Island have none at their own address.
  • Verify any shop with OCM's online location tool, the QR sticker at the door and the universal symbol on packages.
  • Statis, the borough's first legal store, has closed; Verilife on Fordham Road is medical-only.
  • Only Staten Island has fewer stores than the Bronx, but 74 percent of Bronx stores are equity-licensed, the city's highest share.

Legal weed in the Bronx is no longer a novelty, but it is still not everywhere. As of late August 2026, state licensing records show 23 open adult-use dispensaries in the borough: 17 operating under Conditional Adult-Use Retail Dispensary (CAURD) licenses, the equity license set aside for people with past cannabis convictions and their families, and six under standard retail licenses.

That is up from a single store in summer 2023, but it is still fewer than any borough except Staten Island, and the stores are unevenly spread. They bunch along a handful of corridors: Third Avenue in the South Bronx, Arthur Avenue and Fordham Road in the middle of the borough, East Tremont Avenue and Boston Road out east and north. Meanwhile, neighborhoods like Highbridge, Morrisania, Soundview and City Island do not have a licensed store listed at an address of their own.

Below is every open store, grouped by area, with license type, opening date and what each store offers for delivery. Hours change often, so check each store's site first. For deeper write-ups on individual shops, see our Bronx dispensary reviews.

How we built this map

Every store below is open under a license issued by the Office of Cannabis Management (OCM), the state regulator, and every one can be checked against the state's own verification list. Delivery zones, minimums and hours shift often, so treat the delivery notes as a starting point and confirm your address at checkout.

A quick timeline, counted from the list: one of today's open stores opened in 2023, 11 opened in 2024, seven in 2025 and four so far in 2026. The newest, Upscale Cannabis in Pelham Gardens, opened Aug. 7.

The East Bronx has the most stores and the most recent growth: three of this year's four openings landed here. Hush, the borough's second legal store, is now its longest-running, and it nearly did not survive a fight over school-distance measurements because it sits across from P.S. 89. A state law Gov. Kathy Hochul signed on Feb. 12, 2026, grandfathered more than 150 stores approved under mistaken measurements, and Hush stayed open. On East Tremont, Say Less and Victory now operate within a few blocks of each other; we covered that Throggs Neck neighbor fight separately.

How to make sure a store is actually legal

Plenty of Bronx smoke shops still sell THC products that never passed state testing, and some look convincing. In June, an unlicensed Pelham Gardens shop called Indica Diamond allegedly cut the city's padlock off its door and resumed sales before the Sheriff's Office seized more than 800 pounds of product; here is what happened at Indica Diamond. Three checks take less than a minute:

  1. Search the state list. OCM's Dispensary Location Verification page, on the agency's website, lists every licensed storefront.
  2. Scan the sticker. Licensed stores post an OCM QR verification sticker near the entrance.
  3. Read the package. Legal products carry New York's universal cannabis symbol and a QR code that links to lab results.

If a shop fails those checks, you can report it through OCM's online incident form at cannabis.ny.gov/report-an-incident. New to all of this? Start with our first-time buyer's guide.

Know Before You Go

Bring a valid photo ID; every store checks that you are 21 or older. Expect 13 percent in state and local tax at the register. You can carry up to 3 ounces of flower and 24 grams of concentrate in public. No consuming inside the store, in parks or in any vehicle, and never drive high. Our guide to where you can legally smoke in the Bronx covers the rest.

Closed, medical-only and coming soon

Gone: Statis Cannabis Co.

The Bronx's first legal dispensary, Statis Cannabis Co., opened at 817 E Tremont Ave on July 6, 2023. It has since closed here and relocated to New Rochelle, so older maps showing it are out of date.

Medical only: Verilife

Verilife at 25-27 W Fordham Rd is a medical dispensary. It serves registered patients in the state's medical program, not adult-use shoppers.

Licensed or advancing, not yet open

  • Monarch NYC, 574 W 235th St (Riverdale area)
  • Green Heights, 349 E Gun Hill Rd
  • Thurston Hall, 2438 Grand Concourse
  • I Bud You NY Inc., 1446 Williamsbridge Rd, which Community Board 10 voted 29-0 to recommend on April 16, 2026. Board votes are advisory; the state makes the licensing call.

Statewide, the pipeline is still moving. The Office of Cannabis Management's July data counted 683 adult-use dispensaries open across New York as of June, and the Cannabis Control Board continues to approve new licenses at its monthly meetings.

Twenty-three stores for about 1.4 million people: the Bronx has fewer legal counters than every borough but Staten Island.

That works out to roughly one licensed store for every 61,000 Bronx residents. The borough does lead the city on one measure: 74 percent of its stores hold CAURD equity licenses, the highest share of any borough. For the longer view, read our Bronx cannabis scorecard.

Frequently asked questions

How many licensed dispensaries are open in the Bronx?

As of late August 2026, state licensing records show 23 adult-use dispensaries open in the Bronx. Seventeen hold CAURD equity licenses and six hold standard retail licenses; the newest, Upscale Cannabis in Pelham Gardens, opened Aug. 7.

How can I tell if a Bronx weed store is legal?

Search the store on OCM's Dispensary Location Verification page, look for the OCM QR verification sticker near the entrance, and check that products carry New York's universal cannabis symbol and a QR code to lab results.

What was the first legal dispensary in the Bronx?

Statis Cannabis Co. opened at 817 E Tremont Ave on July 6, 2023. It has since closed its Bronx store and relocated to New Rochelle, making Hush in Allerton the borough's longest-running open store.

Which Bronx dispensaries deliver?

Hibernica, Guardian Wellness, SESH NYC, Smoking Scholars, Hush, Bleu Leaf and Two Buds offer delivery, and Buddiez delivers to parts of NYC. Zones, minimums and hours vary, so confirm your address at checkout.
