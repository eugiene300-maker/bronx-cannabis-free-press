Late-Night Munchies in the Bronx: Cheap Eats Rated for Cannabis Lovers

From 3 a.m. yaroa under the 4 train to a same-block patty near Yankee Stadium, here's where the Bronx eats after dark.

Key takeaways

  • Los Munchies on Jerome Avenue lists a 3 a.m. close, the latest on our list, and earns 5/5.
  • Eating Tree at 892 Gerard Ave shares a block with licensed CONBUD Yankee Stadium.
  • Licensed delivery only runs during a dispensary's hours, so buy before you get hungry.
  • My Bud 420 in Van Nest lists hours to 1 a.m.; most Bronx stores close far earlier.

It's 11:40 p.m. The 4 train rattles over Jerome Avenue, the dispensary closed hours ago, and your stomach has opinions. Every Bronx cannabis consumer knows this moment. The answer is not a gas-station sandwich. The answer is knowing, in advance, which kitchens in this borough keep the lights on and which ones are worth the trip.

This is the Free Press guide to late-night and cheap eats for the 21+ crowd, from Dominican street food under the el to a Riverdale deli that has been slicing since 1953. Every stop gets a Friendliness rating, and every rating comes with the same reminder: eat out, consume at home.

How the Friendliness Index Works

Ratings run from 1 to 5 leaves, written as "Friendliness: 4/5," and they are editorial scores built from public information, not staff visits. We weigh five things: the vibe; late hours, but only where a business publishes them; munchie value, meaning flavor per dollar and how well it travels; proximity to a licensed dispensary on the state's license list; and whether consuming nearby is legal. That last one never changes the plan: city rules ban cannabis use in restaurants, bars, outdoor dining areas and parks. Full methodology: our Cannabis Friendliness Index.

Plan the Buy Before the Hunger

The biggest late-night mistake happens hours earlier. Most Bronx dispensaries close well before the kitchens below do, and under the state's delivery rules, licensed delivery can only run during a dispensary's own hours. A few things worth knowing:

  • My Bud 420 at 1798 E Tremont Ave in Van Nest posts hours to 1 a.m., the latest of any licensed store in the borough.
  • SESH NYC in Woodlawn Heights offers same-day North Bronx delivery until 11 p.m.
  • The dispensary near The Hub Buddiez Bronx Cannabis Dispensary at 2935 Third Ave (a Free Press sponsor) is open 9 a.m. to 8 p.m. Monday through Saturday and 10 a.m. to 7 p.m. Sunday, so shop on your way home, not at midnight.

Hours change. Always check the store's own site, and see our Bronx delivery hub for who lists delivery to your block.

The Late-Night Heavy Hitters

Los Munchies (3421 Jerome Ave; 396 E Tremont Ave; 1354 Edward L Grant Hwy)

A family-owned Dominican street-food shop with a name that is basically a mission statement. The Los Munchies menu runs to yaroa (layers of seasoned meat, melted cheese and sauce over your choice of side), mofonguitos (bite-size fried plantain cups filled with meat and cheese), loaded hot dogs, empanadas and salchipapas. The Jerome Avenue location, under the 4 train, lists a 3 a.m. close; the East Tremont and Highbridge shops on Edward L. Grant Highway list 2 a.m.

Friendliness: 5/5. The latest hours on this list, maximum munchie value, and Bleu Leaf, the licensed store at 2034 Jerome Ave in Morris Heights, is on the same avenue, farther south. Get it to go.

Jimbo's Hamburger Palace (912 Soundview Ave; 690 Melrose Ave; 232 Willis Ave; 4137 White Plains Rd)

Four Bronx locations of a no-frills burger counter, spread from Soundview to Mott Haven to the North Bronx. The Melrose Avenue shop is in the same neighborhood as Buddiez, and the Willis Avenue spot is in Mott Haven, home to CONBUD Bronx at 2412 Third Ave. Hours vary by location, so call ahead.

Friendliness: 4/5. A greasy, reliable burger is the platonic munchie. Points off only because we can't confirm late hours across all four.

Eating Tree (892 Gerard Ave)

A Jamaican spot near Yankee Stadium, and a two-address coincidence worth knowing: CONBUD Yankee Stadium, a licensed CAURD store, sits at 898 Gerard Ave on the same block.

Friendliness: 5/5. Proximity doesn't get better than same-block, and Jamaican food travels beautifully. On game days, expect crowds.

Golden Krust (3812 White Plains Rd)

The Caribbean bakery chain was founded in the Bronx in 1989, and a patty remains the most portable, affordable hunger fix in the borough. The White Plains Road location sits under the 2/5 el in the North Bronx, the same general stretch where Two Buds, the Bronx's first Jamaican-owned dispensary, operates at 696 E 241st St in Wakefield.

Friendliness: 4/5. Unbeatable price-to-satisfaction ratio and a real Bronx origin story.

The Neighborhood Plates

El Nuevo Bohío Lechonera (791 E Tremont Ave)

A lechonera is a roast-pork shop, and a plate of lechón and rice is the kind of meal that turns a couch into a food coma. A bit of history: 817 E Tremont Ave, just up the street, was Statis Cannabis Co., the Bronx's first legal dispensary, which opened July 6, 2023, and has since closed and relocated to New Rochelle. My Bud 420 is farther east on the same avenue.

Friendliness: 4/5. Big portions, big flavor. Hours unpublished here, so check first.

Brisas del Caribe (1207 Castle Hill Ave)

A Castle Hill spot for Caribbean comfort food, the kind of plate that holds up on the ride home. Its newest neighbor is Weed Land, a CAURD dispensary that opened June 15, 2026, just up the avenue at 1260 Castle Hill Ave.

Friendliness: 4/5. Same-avenue proximity and serious takeout value.

Louie & Ernie's Pizza (1300 Crosby Ave, Schuylerville)

A beloved East Bronx pizzeria tucked into Schuylerville. Throggs Neck's two licensed stores, Say Less at 3764 E Tremont Ave and Victory Dispensary at 3653 E Tremont Ave, are nearby; read our Throggs Neck dispensary story for that saga.

Friendliness: 4/5. A whole pie is a whole night sorted.

Liebman's Deli (552 W 235th St, Riverdale)

Slicing in Riverdale since 1953, Liebman's is the old-school deli move for the West Bronx. The nearest open licensed store is Frass Box at 3633 Kingsbridge Ave. Monarch NYC, on the same street at 574 W 235th St, holds a license but has not opened.

Friendliness: 3/5. Legendary sandwiches, but not a late-night play.

City Island seafood: Johnny's Reef, Tony's Pier, Lickety Split

Fried seafood at Johnny's Reef (2 City Island Ave) and Tony's Pier, then ice cream at Lickety Split (295 City Island Ave). It's a trip, not a craving, and it demands a plan: take the bus or get a sober ride, because driving high is illegal. Pelham Bay Park and Orchard Beach, on the way, are smoke-free. Hibernica in Pelham Bay lists delivery across the Bronx.

Friendliness: 3/5. High vibe, low convenience.

Night Rules

Buy only from licensed stores; our dispensary map lists all 23 in the Bronx. Don't smoke in restaurants, outdoor seating, cars (even parked) or parks. NYCHA residents: NYCHA bans smoking inside apartments and within 25 feet of buildings. Edibles take time, so start low and go slow. Never drive high. More in our guide to where you can smoke.

The Free Press Take

The Bronx after dark is a food borough first. The smartest late-night strategy is boring: buy legal during business hours, know which kitchen near you stays open, and bring it all home. Los Munchies owns the midnight hour, Eating Tree wins on location, and a Golden Krust patty is still the best dollar-for-dollar decision you can make on an empty stomach.

Frequently asked questions

What Bronx spots are open latest for munchies?

Los Munchies lists a 3 a.m. close at 3421 Jerome Ave and 2 a.m. at its East Tremont and Edward L. Grant Highway shops. Check hours before heading out.

Can I get weed delivered in the Bronx late at night?

Only during a licensed dispensary's operating hours, under state delivery rules. SESH NYC lists North Bronx delivery until 11 p.m.; confirm your zone with the store.

Which Bronx dispensary is open latest?

My Bud 420 at 1798 E Tremont Ave lists hours to 1 a.m. Hours change, so verify on the store's site.

Can I smoke at a restaurant's outdoor tables?

No. NYC bans cannabis smoking and vaping in restaurants and outdoor dining areas. Take your food home.
