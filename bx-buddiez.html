Buddiez Bronx Cannabis Dispensary

A licensed Bronx dispensary at 2935 Third Avenue, three blocks from The Hub — hours, map, directions and what's on the menu.

Buddiez Bronx Cannabis Dispensary is the licensed adult-use cannabis dispensary that sponsors The Bronx Cannabis Free Press. It sits at 2935 Third Avenue in Melrose — the heart of the South Bronx, three blocks north of The Hub — and it describes itself as "created for the Bronx by the Bronx." This page has everything you need to visit: address, hours, map, directions from every corner of the borough, and what's on the menu.

How to get to Buddiez from anywhere in the Bronx

From The Hub / 149th Street

Take the 2 or 5 to 3 Av–149 St. Walk north on Third Avenue about three blocks; Buddiez is between E. 152nd and E. 153rd streets.

From Yankee Stadium & the Concourse

Take the 4 to 149 St–Grand Concourse and transfer to the 2 or 5 one stop east to 3 Av–149 St, then walk three blocks north on Third Avenue.

From Fordham Road & Arthur Avenue

Catch the Bx15 at Fordham Plaza and ride south on Third Avenue to E. 152nd–153rd streets.

From Mott Haven & Port Morris

Ride the Bx15 north on Third Avenue, or walk up Third past The Hub.

From Hunts Point & Longwood

Take the Bx19 west along Southern Boulevard/E. 149th Street to Third Avenue, then walk three blocks north.

From Kingsbridge & Riverdale

Take the 4 south to 149 St–Grand Concourse, transfer to the 2/5 one stop to 3 Av–149 St.

From Harlem & East Harlem

Take the 5 from 125 St to 3 Av–149 St — you're in the Bronx in minutes.

From Pelham Bay & the East Bronx

Take the 6 to 3 Av–138 St, then the Bx15 north on Third Avenue.

Transit directions are our best guidance; check the MTA app for service changes. If you drive, look for street parking on Third Avenue and side streets and read the signs — and never drive after consuming.

What's on the menu

The menu covers flower, pre-rolls, edibles, vaporizers, concentrates, tinctures, topicals, CBD, beverages and accessories, with 151 brands on its brands page, including the house label and New York names like Bodega Boyz, 5 Boro, MFNY, Hudson Cannabis, Ayrloom, Florist Farms and Off Hours alongside national brands like Jeeter, Stiiizy, Wyld, Wana and Heavy Hitters.

Browse the Buddiez menu and order ahead for pickup, check the daily specials, or join the loyalty program. New to the shop? Start at bxbuddiez.com.

Why Buddiez sponsors the Free Press

A free local paper needs someone to pay the bills. Buddiez underwrites our operating costs so the Free Press can stay free for Bronx readers. In return, it's recognized as our presenting sponsor on every page. What it doesn't get: any say over what we report. Our independent Buddiez review scores the shop on the same rubric we use for every Bronx dispensary, and our sponsorship disclosure spells out the rules.

Buddiez FAQ

Where is Buddiez Bronx Cannabis Dispensary?

2935 Third Ave, Bronx, NY 10455, between E. 152nd and E. 153rd streets in Melrose — about three blocks north of The Hub and the 2/5 trains at 3 Av–149 St.

Is Buddiez Bronx Cannabis Dispensary licensed?

Yes. It holds New York OCM license OCM-CAURD-25-000297, an adult-use conditional retail (equity) license, and opened to the public on Oct. 16, 2025.

What are Buddiez's hours?

Monday through Saturday 9 a.m.–8 p.m., and Sunday 10 a.m.–7 p.m., per the store's website.

Does Buddiez offer delivery or pickup?

You can order ahead online for in-store or curbside pickup, and delivery is available to parts of New York City — confirm your address and the order minimum at checkout.

What should I bring?

A valid government-issued photo ID showing you're 21 or older, plus cash or a debit card.
