The Block

What the Bronx is saying about cannabis this week — the stories, the numbers and our read.

The Block is our running dissection of Bronx cannabis news and the conversation around it — community board votes, City Hall and state announcements, enforcement actions, state data releases and the threads that light up local forums. Each item: what happened, and what it means for you.

How we handle social media

We follow Bronx cannabis conversations on Reddit, X, Facebook groups and Instagram, but we don't quote or name private individuals from social posts without their permission. Instead we report the recurring themes and verify any factual claim against a primary source before it runs here. Public officials' posts may be quoted with a link.

  3. Is enforcement fair? A new sheriff, and federal agents at the old one's door. Read more.

The feed

Pelham Gardens · August 7, 2026

Upscale Cannabis opens in Pelham Gardens

The story: The borough's newest licensed dispensary opened on Fenton Avenue, bringing the Bronx to 23 open adult-use stores — still fewer than every borough but Staten Island.

Our read: Every new legal door in the North and East Bronx shrinks the gray market's reason to exist. Access, not enforcement, is what finally empties the smoke shops.

Citywide · August 26, 2026

Hemp THC cap on the clock for November

The story: A 2025 federal law limits hemp products to 0.4 mg of total THC per container and strips synthetic cannabinoids of hemp status beginning in November 2026. Congress has been debating a delay; none has passed.

Our read: The THCA flower and delta-8 vapes sold in bodegas are running out of legal cover. Licensed, lab-tested cannabis isn't affected.

South Bronx · July 30, 2026

Cannabis tax money graduates 13 South Bronx youth

The story: Community Connections for Youth's nine-month PODER Youth Leadership Collective graduated 13 justice-involved young people, paid for by the state's cannabis community reinvestment grants.

Our read: This is the part of the 13% tax you don't see at checkout — and the best argument for buying legal.

Citywide · July 16, 2026

Federal agents search ex-Sheriff Miranda's home

The story: Federal agents executed a search warrant at the Queens home of the former sheriff who led Operation Padlock. No arrests or charges have been announced.

Our read: The raids that padlocked hundreds of Bronx storefronts are now under a microscope. We'll follow any court filings.

Concourse · July 9, 2026

Hip Hop Museum opening slides to spring 2027

The story: The Universal Hip Hop Museum pushed its full opening to spring 2027. Its Culture Lab at Bronx Terminal Market stays open Wednesday through Saturday.

Our read: One more year of waiting — but the Culture Lab is a solid Saturday stop in the meantime.

Statewide · July 2, 2026

State data: legal prices down about 7% this year

The story: New York counted 683 adult-use dispensaries as of June, with $895.4 million in sales through the first half of the year and per-unit prices down roughly 7% since January.

Our read: Good for your wallet, hard on small shops. Loyalty programs and first-time deals are where the real savings show up.

Pelham Gardens · June 10, 2026

Indica Diamond cuts its padlock, gets resealed

The story: The Sheriff's Office says the Pelham Gardens shop at 1334 E. Gun Hill Rd. reopened after being sealed; deputies seized more than 800 pounds of product and resealed it.

Our read: Despite the name on the awning, it was never a licensed dispensary. Look for the state verification sticker before you buy.

Citywide · May 29, 2026

Mamdani names Edwin Raymond sheriff

The story: The mayor replaced Anthony Miranda with Edwin Raymond, a retired NYPD lieutenant and whistleblower. Unlicensed-cannabis enforcement stays among the office's duties.

Our read: New leadership, same law. The question for Bronx blocks is style: fewer sweeps, more cases?

Mott Haven · May 18, 2026

Bud-Zotic raided a third time

The story: Sheriff's deputies and federal Homeland Security Investigations agents seized more than 500 pounds from the Timpson Place shop and 300-plus pounds from a van; two people were arrested.

Our read: Three raids in under two years tells you how profitable — and how untested — the gray market still is.

Pelham Parkway · May 12, 2026

Eviction uncovers 299-plant grow on Pelham Parkway

The story: A city marshal serving an eviction found a five-room hydroponic grow with illegally rewired power in a Pelham Parkway South apartment.

Our read: Illegal grows aren't a victimless hobby — rewired power is a fire risk for everyone in the building.

Throggs Neck · April 22, 2026

Throggs Neck neighbors push back on a second shop

The story: Residents objected to Victory Dispensary opening about a block from Say Less on East Tremont Avenue; the owners promised armed security and ID scanning.

Our read: Two shops on one block is a first for the East Bronx. Competition usually means better prices — and a real test of neighborhood tolerance.

Pelham Parkway / Morris Park · April 16, 2026

CB10 votes 29–0 for a Williamsbridge Road shop

The story: The same board that split 13–10 on Victory voted unanimously to recommend a license at 1446 Williamsbridge Rd.

Our read: Siting fights are about the block, not the board. Show up at your community board meeting if you want a say.
