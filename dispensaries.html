Bronx Dispensary Reviews & Directory

Every licensed dispensary in the Bronx, verified against state records — with our reviews, delivery notes and what to know before you go.

The Bronx has 23 licensed adult-use dispensaries open as of late August 2026 — 17 of them equity (CAURD) licensees, the highest share of any borough. Every store on this page is checked against state licensing records. If a shop isn't here, check the state's dispensary verification tool before you buy.

How we review dispensaries

Our rubric scores six things: license & compliance, menu depth, value & deals, access & convenience, customer sentiment, and community, built on state records, menus, customer feedback and transit access. "Snapshot" entries are short verified profiles; full reviews get a score. Our sponsor, Buddiez, is scored on the same rubric as everyone else. Read the full methodology.

Also good to know

  • Statis Cannabis Co. opened July 6, 2023 at 817 E. Tremont Ave. as the Bronx's first legal dispensary; it has since closed and relocated to New Rochelle.
  • Verilife on West Fordham Road is a medical-only dispensary — you need a NY medical cannabis certification.
  • Coming soon: licensed-but-not-open locations include Monarch NYC (574 W. 235th St.), Green Heights (349 E. Gun Hill Rd.) and Thurston Hall (2438 Grand Concourse).

Store hours, delivery zones and menus change. Always confirm on the shop's own site before you go.
